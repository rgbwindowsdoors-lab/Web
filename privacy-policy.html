Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains what personal information RGB Windows & Doors collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It applies to this website and to the ways you contact us.

1. Who we are

RGB Windows and Doors ("RGB Windows & Doors", "we", "us") replaces and installs windows and exterior doors for homeowners in North Bay and surrounding areas.

Privacy Contact: RGB Windows & Doors, rgbwindowsdoors@gmail.com. Please use this email for any question or request about privacy or your personal information.

2. Information you choose to give us

You may contact us by phone, by email, through our social media pages, or through a form in one of our Facebook or Instagram ads (Meta Lead Ads), if we use them. When you do, you may give us:

  • your name;
  • your phone number and email address;
  • the address of the home where the work would be done;
  • details about your project, such as the number and type of windows or doors.

You decide what to share. We only ask for what we need to answer you and to quote, measure and install your project.

3. Information collected when you visit our website

Necessary cookie. We use one cookie to remember your cookie choice so we do not ask you on every page.

Optional cookies, only with your permission. Analytics and marketing cookies are off by default. They are used only if you turn them on in the cookie banner or in Cookie Settings:

  • Analytics (Google Analytics 4, loaded through Google Tag Manager) helps us understand how the website is used.
  • Marketing (Meta Pixel) helps us measure the results of our Facebook and Instagram ads.

If you allow them, these tools may collect information such as the pages you visit, how you interact with the website (for example, clicks), your type of device and browser, and online identifiers stored in cookies.

4. How we use your information

  • to answer your questions and prepare quotes;
  • to schedule measurements and installations and to provide warranty service;
  • to understand and improve our website, only if you allow analytics cookies;
  • to measure our advertising, only if you allow marketing cookies.

6. Your choices and withdrawing consent

When you first visit, you can choose Accept All, Reject Optional or Customize. Your choice is saved for 12 months, after which we will ask again. You can change your choice at any time with the link at the bottom of every page.

If you withdraw your consent, optional tracking on this website stops and the page reloads. Analytics and marketing cookies stored for our website are deleted where this is technically possible. Information that was already collected before you withdrew consent, and cookies stored by Google or Meta on their own websites, are not removed by this step. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

7. Sharing your information

If you allow analytics or marketing cookies, the information described in section 3 is processed by Google and Meta under their own privacy policies. These companies may store and process information outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be subject to the laws of those countries.

If you contact us through a Facebook or Instagram ad form, the information you enter is also handled by Meta.

We may also share personal information when required by law.

8. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only as long as needed to answer your request, provide our services, meet our warranty obligations and comply with the law. When it is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous.

9. Access, correction and questions

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent, by emailing rgbwindowsdoors@gmail.com. If you have a concern about how we handle your information and we cannot resolve it, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

10. Children

Our website is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
